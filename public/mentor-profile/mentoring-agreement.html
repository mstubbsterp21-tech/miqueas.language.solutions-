Mentoring Agreement

Simple Mentoring Agreement

Use these questions to talk through how you will work together. Keep your answers brief, honest, and practical.

Privacy: Nothing entered on this page is sent to MLS. Your draft is stored only in this browser until you clear it. Use “Print / Save PDF” when you are ready to keep or share a copy.
Professional and ethical practice: Mentoring conversations should respect the NAD-RID Code of Professional Conduct, applicable law, and workplace policies. Interpreting samples and discussions must be de-identified unless proper permission has been obtained. Mentoring supports professional growth; it does not replace independent professional judgment, supervision, legal advice, or mental-health care.

01 Goals

What would you like to accomplish through this mentoring relationship?

How will we know that progress is being made?

02 Roles & Expectations

What do you expect from your mentoring partner?

What will each of us do to help this relationship succeed?

03 Meetings & Communication

How and when will we usually meet or communicate?

What should we do if a meeting needs to change or be canceled?

04 Ground Rules & Boundaries

What guidelines will help us work well together?

What boundaries are important for us to respect?

05 Confidentiality

What information should stay private between us?

Are there any situations when information may need to be shared with someone else?

06 Accountability & Follow-Through

How will we keep track of commitments or action steps?

What should we do if one of us is not able to follow through?

07 Feedback & Challenges

How would you like to give and receive feedback?

How will we handle concerns, misunderstandings, or other challenges?

08 Moving Forward

What is one action each of us will take before our next meeting?

When and how will we review this agreement and make changes if needed?

Agreement

We have discussed these questions and agree to use this document as a guide for our mentoring relationship. We understand that we may revise the agreement together as our needs or goals change.

If your program requires handwritten or verified electronic signatures, print or save this document and complete signatures using the method your program accepts.
