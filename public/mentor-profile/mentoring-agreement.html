Mentoring Agreement

Through Miqueas Language Solutions LLC (MLS), we will choose mentoring goals and activities together and track them in a separate working plan. The mentor offers guidance within their experience; the mentee remains responsible for professional decisions. Mentoring does not establish employment or supervision, authorize practice, or guarantee certification, exam results, or work.

Session arrangements

Our responsibilities

A collaborative relationship works best when we each know what we can expect from one another.

Mentor Responsibilities

  • Listen to your priorities and build on your strengths, experience, and learning preferences.
  • Help you set realistic goals and choose activities that support them.
  • Prepare for sessions and review materials by our agreed deadlines.
  • Give specific, respectful feedback, explain my observations, and make space for your perspective.
  • Support your self-assessment and independent decision-making.
  • Follow through on commitments and acknowledge when your needs fall outside my expertise.

Mentee Responsibilities

  • Take an active role in identifying your goals and communicating the support you need.
  • Arrive prepared, bring questions, and submit agreed materials on time.
  • Reflect on your work, consider feedback, and share your perspective.
  • Complete agreed practice between sessions or communicate when adjustments are needed.
  • Speak up when something is unclear or an approach is not helping.
  • Take responsibility for your professional decisions and follow-through.

Shared Commitments

  • Treat each other with respect and protect confidential information.
  • Agree on communication preferences, access needs, and boundaries.
  • Ask questions, disagree respectfully, and request a different approach when needed.
  • Address concerns directly and without blame.
  • Review progress together and adjust goals or expectations as needs change.

Terms and acknowledgment

Confidentiality and recording

Keep goals, feedback, reflections, and materials private. Share only with permission or when required by law or a binding professional obligation; a serious, immediate safety concern may require limited disclosure. Give notice when legally and safely possible.

Use simulated or appropriately authorized, de-identified practice materials and follow applicable professional and workplace rules. Agree on secure storage, access, and deletion in the working plan. Disclose overlapping agency or assessment roles; unrelated screening, employment, or promotional use requires separate permission.

This agreement does not authorize recording. Recording, transcription, AI summaries, or third-party observation require separate, specific consent before they begin.

Reviewing or ending mentoring

Either person may request changes, pause, or end mentoring through the agreed contact method. No one must continue a relationship that no longer meets their needs. Document changes and have both people acknowledge them. Confidentiality continues after mentoring ends; fees and recordings follow their separate terms.

Fees, if any

Record “Free” or reference separate written fee terms. Agree to payment, cancellation charges, and refunds in writing before paid services begin. A blank field creates no fee obligation.

Acknowledgment

We have reviewed this agreement together, had an opportunity to ask questions, and agree to the responsibilities and arrangements recorded here. We will revisit our working plan together. Each person will receive a completed copy.
