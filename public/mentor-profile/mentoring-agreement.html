Mentoring Agreement

Purpose

This agreement sets expectations for a collaborative mentoring relationship through Miqueas Language Solutions LLC (MLS). We will choose goals and activities together and track them in a separate working plan.

The mentor offers guidance within their experience. The mentee remains responsible for professional decisions. Mentoring does not establish employment or supervision, authorize practice, or guarantee certification, exam results, or work.

Our responsibilities

The mentor will: listen, help set realistic goals, prepare for sessions, offer specific and respectful feedback, follow through, and explain limits in expertise.

The mentee will: communicate priorities and needs, arrive prepared, consider feedback, complete agreed practice, and speak up when plans or expectations need to change.

Both will: respect each other’s time, access needs, perspectives, and boundaries; invite questions and honest feedback; and address concerns without blame.

Session arrangements

Attendance and preparation

Arrive on time with agreed materials. Give as much notice as possible if a session needs to change. Discuss missed commitments and agree on a realistic next step.

Fees, if any

Record “Free” or identify separate written fee terms below. Any payment, cancellation charge, or refund terms must be agreed in writing before paid services begin. A blank field creates no fee obligation.

Confidentiality and recording

Keep goals, feedback, reflections, and materials private. Share only with permission or when required by law or a binding professional obligation; a serious, immediate safety concern may require limited disclosure. Give notice when legally and safely possible.

Use simulated or appropriately authorized, de-identified practice materials and follow applicable professional and workplace rules. Agree on secure storage, access, and deletion in the working plan. Disclose overlapping agency or assessment roles; unrelated screening, employment, or promotional use requires separate permission.

This agreement does not authorize recording. Recording, transcription, AI summaries, or third-party observation require separate, specific consent before they begin.

Reviewing or ending mentoring

Either person may request changes, pause, or end mentoring through the agreed contact method. Discuss concerns respectfully and, where useful, identify next steps or referrals. No one must continue a relationship that no longer meets their needs.

Document changes and have both people acknowledge them. Confidentiality continues after mentoring ends. Any fees or refunds follow separate written terms; recordings remain subject to their separate consent.

Acknowledgment

We have reviewed this agreement together, had an opportunity to ask questions, and agree to the arrangements recorded here. We will develop and revisit our goals and working plan together. Each person will receive a completed copy.
