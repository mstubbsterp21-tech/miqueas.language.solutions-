01 Relationship Details and Purpose

This agreement guides a collaborative mentoring relationship through Miqueas Language Solutions LLC (MLS). Mentoring supports the mentee’s interpreting skills, reflection, ethical decision-making, and professional development. The focus, duration, and activities will be agreed upon together.

The mentor provides guidance within their experience and competence. The mentee remains responsible for professional decisions and assignment acceptance. Mentoring does not establish employment, supervision, certification, or authorization to practice, and does not guarantee examination results, credentials, placement, or employment. Any formal assessment or separate service must be identified and agreed to separately.

Before Session One — Quick Setup

02 Mentor Commitments

  • Listen to the mentee’s priorities, recognize their strengths and experience, and work together to set realistic goals.
  • Prepare for sessions and review agreed materials when provided by the agreed deadline.
  • Offer honest, specific, respectful feedback tied to observable work; invite the mentee’s perspective and support self-assessment.
  • Suggest relevant resources, encourage appropriately challenging practice, and follow through on agreed commitments.
  • Respect communication preferences, access needs, boundaries, and confidentiality.
  • Identify limits in expertise, disclose relevant conflicts or overlapping roles, and discuss referrals or adjustments when appropriate.

03 Mentee Commitments

  • Take an active role in choosing goals, asking questions, and communicating learning and access needs.
  • Arrive prepared and on time, provide agreed materials by the agreed deadline, and communicate when plans change.
  • Consider feedback thoughtfully, share a different perspective when needed, and complete mutually agreed practice or reflection.
  • Use only materials that may appropriately be shared and protect consumer, colleague, employer, and client information. If workplace observation is proposed, arrange required permissions in advance and explain the mentor’s role to affected participants.
  • Take responsibility for professional judgment, choices, and follow-through, and raise concerns about the mentoring relationship promptly.

04 Goals, Practice, and Progress

Draft what you know before session one; treat goals and preferences as proposals until discussed together. It is fine to leave an item “To discuss.”

05 Scheduling, Fees, and Follow-Through

Both people will give as much notice as reasonably possible when a session needs to change. If a commitment is missed, they will discuss what happened and agree on a workable next step. Repeated missed sessions or an ongoing mismatch in availability may lead to a revised plan, pause, or end to mentoring.

No fee, cancellation charge, or payment obligation is created by an uncompleted field. Any paid arrangement must be agreed in writing before the related services begin.

06 Confidentiality and Professional Boundaries

Both people will protect private reflections, feedback, goals, and shared materials. Information may be disclosed with the affected person’s permission or where disclosure is required by applicable law or a binding professional obligation. A serious, immediate safety concern may also require limited disclosure. Any disclosure should be limited to what is necessary, with notice when legally and safely possible.

Use de-identified or simulated interpreting examples whenever possible. Remove names and other identifying details; de-identification alone does not override workplace rules, ownership, or permission requirements. Obtain appropriate authorization before sharing restricted materials. Mentoring discussions should respect the NAD-RID Code of Professional Conduct and applicable workplace policies.

Mentoring feedback and materials will not be used for marketing, public posting, or unrelated employment or screening decisions without separate permission. Discuss any overlapping agency, contracting, or assessment role before proceeding.

07 Recording and Observation

Signing this agreement does not authorize recording. Recording, transcription, AI meeting summaries, and third-party observation require a separate, specific consent or addendum before they begin.

That addendum must identify the purpose, information captured, observers, platform, sharing method, retention or deletion date, and how requests to stop future recording or withdraw participation will be handled. Public or promotional use requires separate permission.

For a practicum that requires recording, review its recording and observation terms separately before accepting that particular opportunity. Previously completed application acknowledgments do not authorize additional uses beyond their stated scope.

08 Reviewing, Pausing, or Ending Mentorship

Either person may request a change, pause, or end to mentoring by notifying the other through the agreed communication channel. No one is required to continue a relationship that no longer meets their needs. Where practical, discuss outstanding action steps and useful referrals. Any remaining payment or refund matters follow the separately agreed fee terms.

Confidentiality and restrictions on sharing materials continue after mentoring ends. Existing recordings remain subject to their separate consent terms and applicable requirements. Any changes to this agreement should be documented and acknowledged by both people.
