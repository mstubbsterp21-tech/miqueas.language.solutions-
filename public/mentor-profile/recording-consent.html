Recording consent

Consent to Record and Share Mentoring Sessions

Use Print / Save Blank PDF to create a blank template, then upload it to your electronic signature platform. Complete all arrangement details there before sending it to the mentee for review and signature. Entries made on this webpage do not appear in the PDF. Both people should receive a completed, signed copy before recording begins.

People and sessions covered

Recording arrangement

Complete every field before signing. Identify the specific sessions, purpose, and viewers; use “None” where appropriate. Any later expansion of the sessions, purpose, or audience requires new consent.

What you’re agreeing to

This consent covers only the sessions and purpose identified above. A recording may capture your face, voice, signing, practice interpretations, questions, and feedback. Use simulated or appropriately authorized practice materials and leave identifying consumer details out of the session. Recording begins only after both permissions below are given and this form is signed. The mentor will announce when recording starts and will pause or stop it when you ask.

Who can view the recordings?

The mentor will upload recordings to YouTube as unlisted videos and share links only with the authorized viewers listed above. Unlisted does not mean private: anyone who obtains a link can view or reshare it, and adding an unlisted video to a public playlist may make it appear in search results. Public posting, promotional use, or use for another purpose requires separate permission.

Before sharing a link, the mentor will obtain each viewer’s agreement to keep the recording and session information confidential and not download, screen-record, copy, forward links, or add the video to a public playlist. Viewers may use the material only for the purpose stated above. These rules reduce risk but cannot guarantee that unauthorized copying or sharing will not occur.

Storage and deletion

The mentor will restrict access to copies under the mentor’s control to the authorized viewers and protect accounts used to store recordings. By the deletion date listed above, the mentor will remove the videos from YouTube and delete other recording copies under the mentor’s control, including copies in the recording platform or local storage. This deadline applies to recordings; both people may retain their signed consent forms.

Your choice and withdrawal

You may ask questions before deciding. Both permissions below are required for the recorded arrangement described here. If you decline either permission, no recording or sharing will take place under this form. Any available unrecorded mentoring option must be identified above before signing; if none is offered, you cannot participate in this recorded arrangement.

You may ask to pause or stop recording during any session. To withdraw consent for future recording or sharing, or to request deletion of existing recordings, contact the mentor at the email listed above. On receiving a withdrawal, the mentor will stop further recording and sharing under this consent. On receiving a deletion request, the mentor will remove the affected videos from YouTube, delete recording copies under the mentor’s control, ask authorized viewers to delete any copies they hold, and confirm the action taken. Material already viewed cannot be unseen, and copies independently saved or shared by others may be impossible to retrieve.

Mentee permissions

Review the completed arrangement details and terms before selecting both permissions and signing.

By signing, the mentee confirms that they have reviewed the completed form and had an opportunity to ask questions. The mentor agrees to follow the recording, viewer access, withdrawal, and deletion commitments in this form.
