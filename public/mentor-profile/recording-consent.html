Practicum recording consent

Consent to Record and Share Mentoring Sessions

The mentor completes the session details before the mentee reviews this form. Both people keep a copy before recording begins. Entries stay on this page until you print or save them.

What you’re agreeing to

The So You Want to Be an Interpreter Mentor practicum requires the mentor to record six live virtual sessions across the practicum. This consent covers only the sessions identified above. A recording may capture your face, voice, signing, practice interpretations, questions, and feedback. Fellow practicum participants will review the mentor’s approach to supporting you. Use simulated or appropriately authorized practice materials and leave identifying consumer details out of the session.

Who can view the recordings?

The mentor will upload recordings to YouTube as unlisted videos and send links to designated practicum participants for observation and feedback. Unlisted videos generally stay out of search results and public channel listings. Anyone who obtains a link may view or reshare it. Public posting, promotional use, or sharing for another purpose requires separate permission.

How long are recordings kept?

In June 2027, the mentor will remove the videos from YouTube and delete copies under the mentor’s control. Copies independently saved or shared by others may be impossible to retrieve.

Your choice

You may ask questions before deciding. Participation in this recorded practicum requires both permissions below. To decline, leave the permissions unsigned and tell the mentor. To stop a recording, tell the mentor during the session. You may contact the mentor listed above to withdraw from future recorded sessions. Previously recorded and shared material may still be used for practicum feedback under these terms, subject to applicable requirements.
